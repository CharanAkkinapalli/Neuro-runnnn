ALGORITHMS

A* PATHFINDING
Drones search a nav-grid built from the level with f = g + h (octile heuristic, 8-way moves). Active laser rows cost +6, low walls ×8, gaps and pillars are blocked. Paths are re-planned every 0.35s toward your predicted position; if you hide, they walk to your last-seen spot and patrol.
ADAPTIVE AI
Repeating a move raises the AI Adapt meter. Lasers and barriers counter your favourite move (jump / slide / crouch) and drones lead their targets.

⏱ Moments of the game
  1. Boot – brain-wireframe menu, orbiting runner.
  2. Play – sector title appears, you spawn on the start platform.
  3. First run – grab chips, jump gaps, ride moving platforms.
  4. Laser gate – time the blink: jump low beams, slide under high ones.
  5. Checkpoint – green pad lights up, respawn point saved.
  6. Drone contact – A* chase starts; slide, dodge, or hide in a violet zone.
  7. AI adapts – repeating moves fills the meter and obstacles counter you.
  8. Exit unlocked – enough chips collected, door opens.
  9. Sector cleared – time + 500 bonus, next sector loads.
  10. Boss – dodge beam and shockwaves, collect 3 shards.
  11. Core destroyed – victory screen, best score saved.
  12. Failure path – HP 0 → lose a life, reboot at checkpoint; no lives → System Failure.

INSTRUCTIONS

You are trapped inside a simulation ruled by a hostile AI. Cross 3 sectors, then destroy the AI core.
• Collect energy chips: the exit door stays locked until you hold enough.
• Jump over low lasers/walls; slide or crouch under high ones. Gaps and moving platforms need sprint-jumps.
• Security drones chase you. Slide into one to wreck it, dodge with Q/E, or crouch inside a violet shadow zone to hide.
• The AI watches you: repeat the same move and it adapts. Obstacles shift to counter your favourite move.
• Drones navigate with A* pathfinding on a grid built from the level: they route around pillars, hop low walls, avoid active lasers, and stop at gaps they can't cross. Press G to show/hide their pink path lines.
• See the Algorithms screen on the main menu for details and toggles.
• Touch green pads for checkpoints. Boss: grab the pink shards, jump the shockwaves, slide/jump the sweeping beam.
